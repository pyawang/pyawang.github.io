## Paper Review: [The Baryonic Faber-Jackson Relation and Fundamental Plane of Galaxy Groups, Elliptical Galaxies, and Dwarf Galaxies]

### Quick Reference
* **arXiv Link:** [abs/2605.26965](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.26965)
* **Key Authors:** [Y. Tian et al.]
* **Primary Field:** [Galaxy Kinematics and dynamics]

---

### The 1-Minute Pitch (TL;DR)
>  The scaling relation changes at MOND acceleration scale; at low acceleration, the BFJR is tight; at high acceleration, the distribution tightly follows Newtonian FP without requiring Dark matter.

---

### 1. Scientific Motivation & Context
* **The Knowledge Gap:** What would be the difference of $Lambda$CDM and Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND) paradigm in galaxy scale? Previous studies are missing sample in some mass gap, and this work is trying to bridge them.
* **Why it Matters:** understanding the precise coupling between visible matter (baryons) and gravitational dynamics across vastly different cosmic scales

### 2. Methodology & Analytical Framework
* **Data & Tooling:** MaNGA Elliptical Galaxies, ATLA3D Elliptical galaxies, Virgo/Fornax cluster dwarfs,  Local supercluster galaxy groups.
* **Core Approximations:** When computing distribution of baryons, they are assuming spherical symmetry.

### 3. Key Findings 
* **Major Results:** 
  1. How baryonic Faber-Jackson Relation and Fundametal Plane change depending on the gravitational acceleration ($g_{bar}$).
  2. Low acceleration: follows tight power law
  3. High acceleration: follows Newtonian virial theorem
* **The Central Figure:** Figure 2 presents the fundamental plane and their fitting results

### 4. Critical Commentary & Future Horizon
* **Strengths:** Covering large range of mass.
* **Limitations:** The velocity dispersion of individual galaxies and galaxy groups are calculated differently, are these apple-to-apple comparison?
