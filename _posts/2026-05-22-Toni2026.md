## Paper Review: [COSMOS-Web galaxy groups: Evolution of red sequence and quiescent galaxy fraction]

### Quick Reference
* **arXiv Link:** [abs/2509.0804](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08040)
* **Key Authors:** [G. Toni et al.]
* **Primary Field:** [Galaxy Evolution, High-redshift]

---

### The 1-Minute Pitch (TL;DR)
>  Red sequence identification and there quiescent fraction measurement for COSMOS-Web field through various approach. Galaxies shows clear quenching after $z<1$.

---

### 1. Scientific Motivation & Context
* **The Knowledge Gap:** How galaxy in groups quench? What is the quiescent fraction? How do we identify these quiescent galaxies? What are the properties of Red Sequence(RS)?
* **Why it Matters:** Fundamental of galaxy evolution. 

### 2. Methodology & Analytical Framework
* **Data & Tooling:** Various method are proposed to identify quiescent galaxies and protoclusters: ML, AMICO algorithm, X-ray observation
* **Core Approximations:** $z=[0,3.7]$

### 3. Key Findings 
* **Major Results:** 
  1. The higher the richness is, the higher the quiescent fraction is. The difference decrease as redshift grows.
  2. X-ray sources are brighter in low z, fainter in high z; brighter in high richness, fainter in low z.
* **The Central Figure:** Figure 9 shows the RS group detected with RS/quiescent fraction along redshift evolution.

### 4. Critical Commentary & Future Horizon
* **Strengths:** COSMOS is one of the data rich fields, this work utilize the abundance of data well.
* **Limitations:** There might be selection effect as higher z sample are showing lower signal to noise ratio, that those decreasing trend in time is in fact lack of detection.
