## Paper Review: [The dual effect of group-scale environments on galaxy quenching during cluster infall: pre-processing and protection]

### Quick Reference
* **arXiv Link:** [abs/2605.23314](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.23314)
* **Key Authors:** [H. Dou et al.]
* **Primary Field:** [Galaxy Cluster, Galaxy Evolution]

---

### The 1-Minute Pitch (TL;DR)
>  Group structure of galaxies may cause a delay in quenching fraction compare to isolated galaxy along infalling timeline.

---

### 1. Scientific Motivation & Context
* **The Knowledge Gap:** What is the path of galaxies infalling to the center of a galaxy cluster, and how would this affect the quenching of galaxies? 
* **Why it Matters:** We might get to know the process before ram pressure stripping (pre-processing).

### 2. Methodology & Analytical Framework
* **Data & Tooling:** Using MCXC cluster sample, selecting X-ray luminous clusters. Galaxy information comes from SDSS-DR18. R(distance to the cluster center)-V(line of sight velocity) diagram gives the infall proxy $d_R$ --> evolution time line. Blooming Tree algorithm is obtained to identify whether galaxies are in cluster, group or isolated.
* **Core Approximations:** 2D velocity projection is the main uncertainty, whether $d_R$ could represent infalling is a open question.

### 3. Key Findings 
* **Major Results:** 
  1. Quiescent process are break down into two stage along the timeline by $d_R>2.5$ and $d_R<2.5$, presenting constant quiescent fraction at early stage and a rapid increase after entering the boundary.
  2. Grouped galaxies are having high quiscent fraction compare to isolated galaxies at early stage ($d_R>3$). However, isolated galaxies catch up the quiscent rate after entering later stage, this is explained as group galaxies are being "protected" by their halo structure.
* **The Central Figure:** Figure 3 defines the stages of galaxy infall ,$d_R$.

### 4. Critical Commentary & Future Horizon
* **Strengths:** Novel observation of protection effect and robust substructure is classified.
* **Limitations:** The infalling process is being treated as a linear process, and 2D projection of observation may related to foreground/background galaxies contaminations.
